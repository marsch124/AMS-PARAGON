# Words in PARAGON

PARAGON uses a few words of its own. Each one is explained here in one or two sentences. Type a word in the search field to find it, or press **Open all** to see the whole list.

## What you aim for

- **Aspiration** — something you steer your life by and never finish, like "Healthy body". It has no date. It is shown with a star, in a deep gold. You find them under **Aspirations**.
- **Goal** — something you want to reach by a date, like "Walk the Kungsleden by August 2027". It is shown with a target (two circles), in gold. A goal can serve an aspiration. You find them under **Goals**. At the top of a goal it is called **Goal with a date**.
- **Target date** — the date a goal should be reached by. Set it with **Set a target date…** at the top of the goal.
- **Measure** — one line on a goal that says how you will know it is reached. Set it with **Set a measure…** at the top of the goal. An aspiration can have one too.
- **This year** and **Long term** — how far ahead a goal with a date reaches. This year is one or two years, Long term is three to ten. The button at the top of the goal says which one it is; press it to change it. It is kept as `horizon:` in the settings block. An aspiration does not have this choice.
- **Reason** — why an aspiration matters to you, in your own words, like "Still walking long days in the fells at 80, with the people I love." It is shown at the top of the aspiration's page, under its name. Write it with **Reason…** (or **Write the reason…** when none is written yet). It comes from the part of the note under `## Why`.
- **No reason written yet** — on an aspiration: nothing is written under `## Why`. The question the Goal template puts there does not count as a reason.
- **Serves** — the link from a piece of work up to what it is for. A project serves a goal. A goal or an area serves an aspiration. Set it with the **Serves…** button at the top of a note.
- **Add a project…** — a button on a goal and on an area. On a goal it makes a new project that already serves the goal. On an area it makes a new project that is already in the area. You stay where you are.
- **Add a goal…** — a button on an aspiration. It makes a new goal that serves the aspiration. The new goal has no target date yet; give it one with **Set a target date…**.

## Your notes

- **PARA** — the four kinds of note the app is built on: Projects, Areas, Resources and Archive.
- **Project** — work with an end, like "Renovate the bathroom". Shown in green. It is finished when you mark it **Done**.
- **Deadline** — the date a project should be finished by. Set it with **Set a deadline…** at the top of the project.
- **Next:** — on a project's box in the **Projects** list: the task to do next. That is the task marked `#next`, or else the first open task. When every task is ticked but a subtask under one of them is still open, it is that subtask. A line you are waiting on somebody else for is never next.
- **Weekly bars** — the eight small green bars on a project's box and at the top of the **Projects** list: the tasks finished in each of the last eight weeks, Monday to Sunday, with this week on the right. On an area's page they are pink and count the area and its projects.
- **No next action** — on a project's box: every task is ticked, or every task that is still open is a line you are waiting on somebody else for, and the project is still active. Mark it done, or write its next task. In the **Weekly review** an area is flagged with it only when nothing is left to do anywhere in it: not in its own text, not in a running or on-hold project in it, and not in a sub-area.
- **No tasks yet** — on a project's box: the project has no tasks at all.
- **Not linked to an aspiration** — a group in the **Projects** list: the projects serve a goal, but that goal has no aspiration above it, or no goal with that name can be found.
- **Icon** — a note's own small picture, drawn on a colour. A project, an area, a goal, an aspiration and a resource can each have one. Choose it with **Icon…** at the top of the note: 134 hand-drawn icons, with a search called **Find an icon**. It is shown in the project's tile in the **Projects** list, in the row of an area or a resource, and instead of the star or the target on a goal or an aspiration. It is kept as `icon:` and `icon-colour:` in the settings block. An icon replaces an emoji from an earlier version.
- **Automatic** — in the icon picker, the colour choice that leaves the tile in the colour of the kind of note: green for a project, pink for an area, gold for a goal (deeper for an aspiration), blue for a resource. It is first among the colours.
- **No icon** — in the icon picker, the choice that takes the icon away. The colour you chose stays. On a goal or an aspiration with no colour chosen, the star or the target comes back.
- **Projects with no goal** — a group in the **Projects** list and in the weekly review: projects with no **Serves** line at all. Not an error.
- **Due after its goal** — a project whose deadline is later than its goal's target date. Shown in orange.
- **Area** — a part of life you keep looking after and that never finishes, like "Health" or "Home". Shown in pink.
- **Sub-area** — an area that is part of another area, like "Yoga" inside "Health". Set it with **Part of…** at the top of the area.
- **Projects in this area** — the list at the top of an area: its projects that are running or on hold, with what each needs next, and its sub-areas under them. Above it, pink weekly bars count the tasks finished in the area and its projects.
- **In area** — the area a project is part of, like "Health". Set it with **In area…** at the top of the project, or on its right-click menu. It is kept as `area:` in the settings block. Only this line puts a project in an area; a `[[Health]]` link in the text does not.
- **Standard** — what "good" looks like in an area, like "Three runs a week, one rest day, a yearly check-up." It is shown at the top of the area's page and under the area's name in step 6 of the **Weekly review**. Write it with **Standard…** (or **Write the standard…** when none is written yet). It is kept under `## Standard` in the note's text.
- **No standard written yet** — on an area: nothing is written under **Standard**. The question the Area template puts there does not count as a standard.
- **Resource** — reference material you want to keep, like a packing list. Shown in blue.
- **Archive** — notes you have put away. Shown in grey. They are kept, just out of the way. On the Mac, the **Move to Archive** button at the top of a note has the small word **Archive** under it [CHECK]. A project or goal that was Done, Missed or Dropped keeps that word and the day it ended.
- **Daily note** — one note for each day. It holds that day's plan, tasks and **Looking back**. PARAGON calls it by its day, like **Thursday 2026-11-12**. Its file is named `20261112.md`.
- **Weekly note** — one note for each week. PARAGON calls it by its week, like **Week 46 · 2026**. Its file is named `2026-W46.md`.
- **Settings block** — the lines between the two `---` at the top of a note, such as `type: project` and `tags: [sport]`. The buttons at the top of the note write into it. Since build 312 it is folded away when you open a note. The line **Settings block hidden** above the text shows it, and the line **Settings block** folds it away again.

## How a note stands

- **Active** — you are working on it. A note with no status line is active.
- **On hold** — not now, but not given up.
- **Done** — finished, and it did what it was for.
- **Missed** — it is over, and it did not happen.
- **Dropped** — you decided not to do it. Shown in grey, not orange, because it was a decision and not a failure.
- **Ended** — the day a project or a goal was marked Done, Missed or Dropped, written as `ended:` in its settings block. A finished project shows it as "Done on 2026-10-21", and its row in **Projects** as "Ended 2026-10-21". In **Projects**, the finished groups put the newest ending first, each box shows its day, and the **Done** heading says how many of its projects ended this year.
- **What is next for this goal?** — the question that comes up when you mark a project Done, Missed or Dropped and its goal is left with nothing working on it. **Add a project…** opens **New** with the goal already chosen, **Mark the goal done** is there only after Done, and **Not now** closes it and changes nothing.

## Tasks

- **Task** or **action** — one line with a tick box, in any note. The two words mean the same thing.
- **How long?** — the small dashed button on the right of a task that has no task time yet. Press it to say how long the task takes. Since build 312 it is shown only in **Start the day** and in **Plan** [CHECK]. In other lists a task shows its time only when it has one.
- **Next action** — the one task in a note you would do next. A note has at most one. It carries the tag `#next`.
- **Due date** — the day a task should be done. Written `>2026-09-29` on the task line.
- **Overdue** — a task whose due date has passed. Its date is shown in red.
- **At** — the time of day a task is due, like `T14:00` after its date on the task line. You set it in the date window, in the line called **At**, or press one of the quick times there. **No time of day** there takes it away. It is not the same as **Task time**, which is how long the task takes. In a note that syncs to Reminders, Apple Reminders rings at that time. **Plan** shows a task with a time as a small grey card at its hour.
- **Task time** — how long a task takes, like `~45m` on the task line. The small button on the right of a task sets it; in **Start the day** and in **Plan** it says **How long?** while there is none [CHECK]. **Use task times** in Settings turns it on and off.
- **Working day ends** — the time in Settings that **Start the day** measures your picks against. 16:00 to begin with. Tasks can still be planned after it; it is a measure, not a limit.
- **Important** — one, two or three `!` on a task line. More marks mean more important.
- **Repeat** — a task that comes back after you tick it, like `@repeat(weekly)`.
- **Subtask** — a task indented under another task.
- **Undo** — a button in the message at the bottom of the window, for about eight seconds, after two changes that happen in one press: moving every overdue task to today or tomorrow, and deleting a line in the Inbox. It puts back what the press changed, and only that: a task you changed in the meantime is left as it is, and the message says so.
- **Waiting for** — a task you have handed to somebody else and are waiting on, like "Waiting for Anna: the quote". It has the tag `#waiting`, or it starts with the words **Waiting for**. Make one with **Waiting for…** on a task's menu. The lines are listed by person under **All actions**: choose **Waiting for** in the switch **Actions | Waiting for** under the title [CHECK]. A waiting line is never your next action. It is also the name of a snippet.
- **Follow-up date** — the day to ask again about a **Waiting for** line. It is the line's ordinary date. A new waiting line gets one a week from today if it had none.
- **Someone** — the heading in the **Waiting for** list for lines that name nobody, with the words **No name given**. A line names somebody when it reads "Waiting for Anna: …", with a colon after the name.
- **Follow up next week** — the small button under a line in the **Waiting for** list. It moves the follow-up date one week on, from the later of its date and today.
- **Not waiting any more** — on the task menu of a waiting line. It takes the words, the tag and the `@since` day off, and keeps the date.
- **@since** — `@since(2026-11-01)` at the end of a waiting line: the day the waiting began. It is how the list can say "waited 5 days". A line without it never says how long.
- **Tag** — a word with `#` in front, like `#travel`, on a task or in a note's `tags:` line. It groups things across notes. See **Tags** under **Tools**.

## Planning your day and week

- **Today** — the tasks due today and the overdue ones, with today's calendar events.
- **Note**, **Start** and **Close** — the small words under the three round buttons at the top of **Today** [CHECK]. **Note** opens today's note, **Start** opens **Start the day**, and **Close** opens **Close the day**.
- **Plan** — the screen with your day on an hour ruler. It has the same name everywhere: the **Plan** row in the Mac's sidebar and the **Plan** tab on the iPhone. The lane inside it where your blocks sit is still called **Time blocks**.
- **Plan block** — an orange block of time you set aside for something, like "09:30–11:00 Deep work". It is a line in the day's daily note, under **Plan**. It stays in PARAGON unless you tick **In Apple Calendar**.
- **Time blocks lane** — the right-hand lane on **Plan**, where your plan blocks sit. Drop a task on an hour here to make a block. A task that has a time of day (**At**) is drawn here too, as a small grey card with a thin border and no orange, so you can tell it from a block. It cannot be moved.
- **Next free time** — on the iPhone, in the small menu that opens when you tap an action in **Plan** [CHECK]. It makes a plan block for the action in the first free time left today.
- **Pick a time…** — in the same menu. You choose the hour of the block yourself [CHECK].
- **Calendar lane** — the left-hand lane on **Plan**. It shows your Apple Calendar events and is read only.
- **Apple Calendar blocks** — the older kind of block, which is a real event in Apple Calendar. Reach them with the calendar button on **Plan**.
- **Now line** — the thin blue line that shows the time right now.
- **Now and Then** — two short lines under the date on **Today**, on a day with a plan. The first starts with **Now** and names the plan block you are in, with a small orange clock button that starts the focus timer on it. The second starts with **Then** and names the next block. They are not the **Now line**, which is the thin blue line across the hours.
- **Focus on this** — starts the focus timer on a task or a plan block. It is at the bottom of the list the **How long?** button opens, and first on a task's right-click menu.
- **Focus timer** — a clock on one task while you work. A bar at the foot of the screen shows it, with a pause button and a stop button. On the Mac the time also shows in the menu bar, on the right among the small icons there.
- **Notification when the time is up** — one notification when you have worked as long as the task's time, or the plan block's length. Paused time does not count. The clock keeps running until you stop it.
- **Worked** — a heading in the daily note where the focus timer writes how long you worked: one line for each stretch, like `- 08:02-08:54 Write the offer`.
- **Week** — **Calendar › Week**: the seven days of a week, with what is due on each.
- **Plan the week** — the title of the week when you open it with the **Week** button in **Plan** on the iPhone [CHECK]. It is the same week as **Calendar › Week**. You stay in the **Plan** tab, and the back arrow returns to your day.
- **Goal for this week** — one line at the top of **Week** that says what the week is about. It is kept in the week's note as `focus:` and shown under the date on **Today**. Step 8 of the **Weekly review** writes it too, for the week you are about to work in.
- **Not planned yet** — the dashed tiles at the top of **Week**: tasks with no date yet. Drag one onto a day to give it that date.
- **Start the day** — the cup button on **Today**, with the word **Start** under it [CHECK]. At the top it shows what you wrote last evening, the goal for the week and today's calendar events. You pick the actions that matter most, and each one goes into today's plan as a block as long as its task time. New actions can be added there with **+**.
- **Close the day** — the moon button on **Today**, with the word **Close** under it [CHECK]. It shows what your finished tasks went towards, what did not happen, and what is to do tomorrow, asks one line about the day, and shows the time you planned next to the time you worked.
- **First** — a mark you put on an action in **Close the day**. The next morning, **Start the day** shows it first in its list, above **Overdue**.
- **Morning reminder** — a notification every morning, at a time you choose in **Settings**. Press it to open **Start the day**.
- **Evening reminder** — a notification once a day, at a time you choose in **Settings**. Press it to open **Close the day**.
- **Looking back** — the part of a daily or weekly note where one line about the day or the week is kept.
- **Planned and worked** — a table at the bottom of **Close the day**. For each thing you planned or worked on today, it shows the time planned next to the time the focus timer measured, and **All of today** at the bottom.
- **Today's plan** (the widget) — a widget for the Mac, found under **Edit Widgets**. The big orange line names the plan block you are in now, or else the next one, with when it is. A small ruler of the next hours shows your plan blocks as orange boxes and your calendar events as purple outlines. Click it to open **Plan**. It is not the same as the **Today** screen or the **Plan** screen.

## Dates and times

- **Date** — PARAGON writes every date the same way: year, month, day, like **2026-11-12**. Where the weekday helps, it comes first, like **Thursday 2026-11-12**: on **Today**, in **Start the day** and **Close the day**, on a day in the **Calendar**, in **Plan** and in **Done**, and as the name of a daily note. The weekday is always in English.
- **Month** — a month alone is written like **2026-11**. It is the heading of the month in the **Calendar**, and it stands above the squares in **Done**.
- **Week 46** — the number of the week in the year. A week runs from Monday to Sunday. The heading over a week shows its first and last day too, like **Week 46 · 2026-11-09 – 2026-11-15**.
- **Time** — always 24 hours, like **14:30**, whatever your device is set to.
- **Today**, **Tomorrow** and **Yesterday** — PARAGON uses these words in place of the date where they fit, like "Due tomorrow" or "Synced yesterday at 21:40".
- **Dates inside a note** — what you or PARAGON write into a note keeps its own form, like `>2026-11-12` on a task. The first line PARAGON writes into a new daily note, like "Thursday, 12 November 2026", is part of the note and is never rewritten.

## Getting things in

- **Inbox** — where new things land before you decide where they belong.
- **Capture** — writing something down quickly, to sort later. It goes to the Inbox. On the iPhone it starts with the round **+** button at the bottom right, just above the tab bar [CHECK]. In **New**, the button for it is called **Inbox**, because that is where the line lands.
- **New** — the screen that adds something: an aspiration, a goal, a project, an area, a resource, or a line for the Inbox. It opens with **+** or ⌘N.
- **Add** and **Save** — a button that adds one thing straight away says **Add**, like **Add** beside a new tag. A screen with fields to fill in ends with **Save**. The app does not say Make or Create for this.
- **File it** — moving an Inbox line into a project or an area. On the iPhone the **File it** button opens the list of your projects and areas at once.
- **Tap**, **Hold**, **Click** and **Right-click** — PARAGON uses the words of the device you hold. On the iPhone and the iPad it says **Tap** and **Hold**. On the Mac it says **Click** and **Right-click**. **Hold** means: press and keep your finger there until a menu opens.

## Looking after the whole

- **Weekly review** — a walk through everything, step by step, once a week. It shows what needs a look.
- **Due for a look** — notes you have not looked at for a while. They are listed at the top of the **Weekly review**. How often each kind is due is set in **Settings › Review rhythm**.
- **Changed today** — in the **Weekly review**, on a project or a goal: something in it was changed today. **Changed 3d ago** means three days ago.
- **Looked at** — when you last looked at an aspiration, a goal or an area, like **Looked at 12 days ago**, or **Not looked at yet** when you never have. It is a button at the top of the note. Press it, then **I looked at it today**, and today's date is kept as `reviewed:` in the settings block.
- **I looked at it today** — says you have just looked at a note, so it leaves **Due for a look** until it is due again. It is in the box the **Looked at** button opens, on the right-click menu of a row in **Aspirations**, **Goals** and **Areas**, and in the **Weekly review**.
- **Look back at last week** — step 7 of the **Weekly review**. It shows a whole week from your notes: its goal, the tasks you finished each day, your evening lines from **Close the day**, the time on the focus timer, and the projects that ended. On Monday and Tuesday it shows the week that has just ended. The heading names the week it shows: **last week**, **this week**, or a week number.
- **How was the week?** — a box in step 7 of the **Weekly review**. You write a line or two about the week, and it is kept in that week's note under **Looking back**. It is not the same as the two questions under **Review** that a weekly note already has.
- **Map** — a drawing of what serves what: aspirations at the top, then goals, areas and projects.
- **All actions** — every open task in the vault, with tick boxes to narrow the list. The switch **Actions | Waiting for** under its title changes between your own actions and the lines you are waiting on [CHECK].
- **Filter** — the button in **All actions** that shows the tick boxes that narrow the list, and folds them away again [CHECK].
- **Search** — find words, with tick boxes for the kind of note and the state of tasks. On the iPhone the search field is at the very top of **Browse** [CHECK].
- **Saved search** — a search you kept, shown as a chip on the **Search** screen.
- **Recent** — the notes you opened last. On the iPhone the newest ones are in a short row at the top of **Browse** [CHECK].
- **Done** (in the sidebar) — a diary of the last 12 weeks. For each day: the tasks you finished, the time on the focus timer, your line from **Close the day**, and what the work went towards. At the top, **Last 12 weeks** shows a small square for each day; the stronger the colour, the more was done. An empty square with an orange dashed edge is a day whose note is still coming from iCloud.
- **Deleted** — notes you deleted. They are kept for 30 days and can be restored.

## Tools

- **Template** — the starting text for a new note of one kind.
- **Snippet** — a short block of text you can put into any note. The **Snippets** list shows each one in plain words, like "Ask (who) to (what), tomorrow": a word in round brackets is a question PARAGON asks you when you use the snippet.
- **Tags** — the list of every tag, with how much of the vault uses it. You can rename, delete or add a tag here.

## Behind the scenes

- **Vault** — the folder on your disk or in iCloud that holds all your notes as plain text files.
- **Not synced** — the words beside the sync ring when no sync has run yet [CHECK]. Before build 312 it said "Not synced yet".
- **Synced … ago** — the words beside the sync ring after a sync. They say how long ago it was, like **Synced 5 min ago** [CHECK]. Before build 312 it was only a time, like "10:55". One tap on the ring (one click on the Mac) opens its menu, with **Sync now** first.
- **Reminders list:** — at the top of a project or an area: the list in Apple Reminders its tasks go to.
- **Reminders sync** — tasks are copied both ways between PARAGON and Apple Reminders. Goals and aspirations never sync.
- **Backup** — a dated copy of the whole vault, made every day and before each sync, kept in tiers (last 5 before-sync copies, one per day for 14 days, one per week for 2 months). Since build 308 a second copy, with pictures and sketches, is made once a day outside the vault: on this device, or in a folder you choose.
- **Conflict copy** — a file named "… (conflict date time).md" beside a note. It holds a second version of the note that PARAGON would not throw away: your text when both devices changed the same line, or a version iCloud had parked.
- **Waiting for iCloud** — notes that iCloud has not put on this device yet. The app says so rather than showing them as missing, with the words **still coming from iCloud** and a button, **Ask iCloud again**. PARAGON fetches them in the background and never waits for them while it starts.
- **Build number** — the number at the foot of the sidebar. It tells you which version of the app you have.
