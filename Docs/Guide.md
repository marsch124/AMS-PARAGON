# How PARAGON works

PARAGON keeps your notes and tasks in one place on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and keeps your tasks in step with Apple Reminders. This guide follows a normal day. Each part says what it is for, then how to use it. "Right-click" on the Mac is "hold" (a long press) on the iPhone and iPad.

## 1. The idea in five lines

- **Aspirations** are what you steer your life by and never finish, like "Healthy body". They have no date.
- **Goals** are things you want to reach by a date, like "Walk the Kungsleden by August 2027". A goal can serve an aspiration.
- **Projects** are work with an end. **Areas** are parts of life you keep looking after, like Health. **Resources** are reference material. The **Archive** holds what is over. Projects and areas can serve a goal.
- Tasks are not kept in a list of their own. A task is one line with a tick box inside a note, and every list in the app is a view of those lines.
- Every note answers one question: what does this serve?

You do not have to fill in all of it. Start with a few projects and the Inbox, and add the upper levels when you want them.

When PARAGON opens, a calm start screen shows the day and what is waiting. Tap it (on the Mac, click it) to go in.

## 2. Catch a thought

Capture is for getting something down in seconds without deciding where it belongs. It lands in the **Inbox**, to be sorted later.

- **iPhone:** the round **+** button at the bottom right of Today, Actions and Inbox. It opens the **New** page already set to **Inbox**.
- **Mac:** Shift-Command-N, or **Inbox** in **New** (Command-N). A capture panel also sits in the menu bar. Under **Settings › Quick capture** you can choose a key that opens a small capture panel over any app. It is off until you choose one.
- **Siri** ("Add to PARAGON") and **Share** are in section 12.

Under the capture field, small dashed chips show what PARAGON understood: a date, a priority, a tag. No chip means that part was not understood. You also choose where it goes (Inbox, today's note or a project) and whether it is a task or a note.

Under the field you also find **Dictate** (speak instead of type), **Scan a list** (photograph a paper list; iPhone and iPad) and, if you paste two lines or more, the question **Make 5 tasks?**. "Already in the Inbox" warns you about the same line twice. In **Settings › Place name** you can switch on a place name at the end of each capture (off at start).

### Sorting the Inbox

Pick a line and four buttons appear: **Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Pick a date** and **File it** (into a project or area). Captured notes sit under **Notes**, where **Make task** turns one into a task. When nothing is left, the screen says **Inbox zero**.

In the Inbox, **Snooze until…** on a line's menu puts it away until a day (on the iPhone it is also at the end of **File it**), and **Select** lets you pick several lines and file them at once. When every line is snoozed, the Inbox says **All lines are snoozed** and when the first comes back; **Bring back** brings them all back.

On the iPhone, **File it** lists the likely places first (since build 331). **Suggested** has up to 2 places whose name shares a telling word with the line. **Recent** has up to 3 places you last moved a task to on this phone. Under them, **All projects and areas** is the whole list, so nothing is hidden. With 6 places or fewer there are no groups. **Move to** on a line's menu has the same groups.

**Search in File it (since build 332).** Before, with many places, **File it** was a long scroll, and a menu cannot hold a field to type in. Now, with more than 6 places, the menu has one new row on top, **Search…**. It opens a small page, **File it**. It shows the words of the line in one small grey line, a search field with the cursor in it, and under it the same three groups. Type a word or two and the places are cut down: every word you type must be somewhere in the name, capitals and accents do not matter ("norrkop" finds "Norrköping"), and names where a word begins with what you typed come first. Tap a row to file the line and close the page. **Return** files it to the first row shown. **Cancel** (Escape on the Mac) closes the page and files nothing. If nothing fits it says "No project or area with these words." With 6 places or fewer there is no **Search…** row.

## 3. Today

**Today** is the screen you live in. It shows the day's calendar events, then what needs you now: overdue tasks, tasks due today, the one next action of each active project, and undated tasks marked important.

Four round buttons sit at the top (since build 332; before, three): **One** opens the page One thing, **Note** opens today's note, **Start** turns Today into a picking list (since build 335; before, it opened a second page), **Close** opens Close the day.

On **Today**, a small card **From your Resources** brings back one Resource note at least 30 days old, with **Open** and **Not today** (Settings › Tasks can turn it off). On the iPhone and iPad, **Settings › Health** can write one line a day into your daily note, like "Steps 8,412 · Walk 35 min" (off at start; PARAGON only reads, it never writes to Health).

### One thing

A list asks you to choose. **One** shows only one task and nothing else. It is the small button with a ring and the figure 1, left of **Note**. It opens the page **One thing**: one task in large type, a small line that says why it is the one and which note it is in (for example "Overdue since Friday · Car"), and three buttons.

Since build 334, when the task leads to an aspiration and you wrote a **Reason…** on it, your reason stands under the task as one quiet line in the aspiration's gold. It reminds you why the task matters. With no reason written there is no line.

- **Done** ticks the task, and the next one comes.
- **Not now** sets the task aside for today, and the next one comes. It is only on this device and nothing is written into the note. The next day the task is offered again.
- **Start focus** starts the focus timer on the task and closes the page. It is not there where a task cannot be focused on.

A tiny line at the foot says "3 more after this", "1 more after this" or "The last one for now". **Close** (top left, Escape on the Mac) leaves everything as it is.

Which task: first the one overdue longest. Then what is due today, with a time first. Then the next action of each project, as **Today** lists them. A task that is done, someday, waiting for someone or dated in the future is never shown. When nothing is left the page says "Nothing for now." If you set tasks aside it says how many and has **Show them again**.

## 4. Picking for the day, Plan, Focus, Close the day

### Picking for the day

This is for deciding each morning what matters most. There is no second page. Press **Start** on Today's band and Today's own list becomes the picking list (since build 335; before, **Start** opened a page called Start the day). A dark strip under the band says **Picking for today** and has a **Done** button.

At the top, a card, **This morning**, shows what you wrote last evening and the goal for the week. Under it is **Add an action for today**, which puts a new thought into the Inbox, dated today, already picked. Anything you marked **First** the evening before comes first, under **Marked first last evening**. Below are the tasks Today already shows: from today's note, next actions, overdue, due today and important with no date. Every one has **Pick** in front of it.

Press **Pick** beside the two or three that matter most. When one is picked, a button appears at the foot, such as **Plan 2 actions**. Each pick becomes a block in today's plan, as long as the task's own time. A task with no time shows nothing at the right; pressing **Pick** on it asks **How long?** first and then picks it. A task you typed into the field is picked at once, with no time; it shows a dashed **How long?** so you can give it one. A task that has a time keeps its time pill. A task already in the plan says **In plan** and its time.

To leave without planning, press **Done** on the strip, press **Start** again, or press Escape on the Mac. The plan button plans and leaves. While you pick, the round + button on the iPhone steps aside and swipes on a task are off.

### Plan

Plan shows your day on an hour ruler. The left lane is your Apple Calendar, read only. The right lane, **Time blocks**, is yours: orange blocks such as "09:30–11:00 Deep work". A block stays inside PARAGON. A task with a time of day appears as a small grey card at its hour, which you cannot move.

- **Mac and iPad:** drag a task from **Actions** onto an hour. It becomes a one-hour block; drag its edge to change the length, right-click to remove it.
- **iPhone:** actions sit as dashed tiles above the hours. Hold one and drag it onto an hour, or tap it and choose **Next free time** or **Pick a time…**. The **Week** button opens **Plan the week**.
- A thin blue line shows the time now. On the Mac, Shift-Command-P opens Plan in a window of its own.

A block says when you mean to do the work. It does not tick the task.

### Focus

The focus timer is a clock on one task while you work. When you stop it, the time is written into today's note, so you can later see how long things really took.

Start it with **Focus on this**, the first line of a task's menu, or swipe a task from the right and press **Focus** (iPhone and iPad). Siri can start it too.

A bar at the foot of the screen shows the time, with pause and stop buttons. The pause button is full orange (since build 334), the stop button stays quiet. Paused time does not count. One notification tells you when the task's time is used up. On the Mac the time also shows in the menu bar.

### Close the day

One page you work down at the end of the day:

- **Did not happen:** tasks still open that were due today or earlier. Each has **Tomorrow** and **Day…** buttons, and one button moves them all to tomorrow.
- **To do tomorrow.** Press **First** on the ones that should come first; tomorrow's picking list on Today shows them at the top.
- **How was today?** One line, if you feel like it, kept in today's note. It never becomes a task.
- **What you did today:** how many tasks you finished and what they went towards.
- **Planned and worked:** time planned next to time the focus timer measured.

**Done** saves your line. Settings can remind you each morning and evening: **Remind me to start the day** and **Remind me to close the day**.

## 5. Tasks

Tick a task by its circle, or swipe it from the left edge. On the iPhone and iPad, swipe from the right to move it to tomorrow. Right-click a task (hold on the iPhone) for its menu.

### Dates and times

**Reschedule** offers Today, Tomorrow, Next Monday, In a week, **Pick a date** and Remove date. **Pick a date** opens the date window, with a line called **At** for a time of day. A task with a time is a fixed point in your day: Plan draws it at its hour and Apple Reminders rings then. **At** is when; **How long** is for how many minutes.

### Plain-word dates

Where you capture or add a task, you can write the date in English words: "Call the bank tomorrow 14:00", "Send the report next Friday", "Water the plants in 3 days". A dashed chip shows what was read, such as **Due tomorrow 14:00**; its small x takes the date away. PARAGON never guesses: a weekday in the middle of a sentence is left alone. "Friday" means the next Friday. Weeks start on Monday.

### Deadlines

A task's date is when you plan to do it. Its **Deadline** is when it must be finished. Set it in the task menu (**Deadline**) or on the **Deadline** page of the date window. The row shows a small red flag and words like "7 days left", "Due today" or "3 days late". Moving the task never moves its deadline. A task is overdue when its date or its deadline has passed. Apple Reminders does not get the deadline.

### Repeats

**Repeat** offers every day, week, 2 weeks, month, 3 months or year, plus **On weekdays…**, **Last day of the month** and **Other…**. **Count from the day I finish it** counts the next one from the day you tick it. Ticking a repeating task makes the next one appear below it.

### Waiting for

A task you have handed to somebody else: choose **Waiting for…** on the task menu and say **Who?**. The line reads "Waiting for Anna: the quote", gets a follow-up date a week away and remembers the day the waiting began.

In **All actions**, the switch at the top has two halves, **Actions** and **Waiting for**. The second lists lines by person and says how long you have waited. **Follow up next week** moves a line's date a week on. **Not waiting any more** turns it back into an ordinary task. A waiting line is never your next action.

### Small steps, energy, moves, places and people

- **Small steps:** a task with subtasks shows a pie in its circle and "2/4". The pie is not a tick. When you tick the last open step, the task is ticked too, and PARAGON says so. Unticking a step does not untick the task.
- **Energy** on the task menu: **Easy**, **Medium**, **Hard**. **All actions** has boxes for them.
- **Moved 5 times:** a task you keep moving to a later day gets this line and a menu: **Do it now**, **Split it**, **Drop it**.
- **Remind me at a place…:** Apple Reminders rings when you arrive or leave. The task must be in a note that syncs. **Your places**, above the search field, offers **Home**, **Work** and the places you used before, with one press. Set Home and Work once in Settings › **Home and Work**.
- **Person…** links a task or a note to someone in your Contacts. **Tags › People** lists everything about one person.

### Important, how long, next

- **Important:** one, two or three exclamation marks.
- **How long** says how many minutes a task takes; Plan uses it for block lengths.
- **Make this the next action** marks the one task you would do next in a project.

Also on the menu: **Rename…**, **Move to** (Inbox, a project or an area), **Block time for this…**. A task indented under another is a subtask.

### Someday

A task you want to keep but not do now: choose **Someday** on the task menu. It loses its date and steps out of **All actions**, until you tick the **Someday** box there. **Not someday any more** on the same menu brings it back. It is the word #someday on the task's line, so you can also type it.

## 6. Notes

A note opens ready to write in. On the iPhone, a pencil button turns writing on and off. While you write, headings grow, bold and italic show as they will look, and finished tasks are struck through.

- **Making a note:** **New** (Command-N) has six buttons: Aspiration, Goal, Project, Area, Resource and Inbox. Choose a kind, name it, and set what applies, such as the goal it serves, its area or its tags. On the Mac, Escape is Cancel and Return saves.
- **The top of a note** has buttons: **Serves…** (which goal or aspiration it is for), **In area…**, **Tags…** and **Icon…** (a small picture of its own). The settings behind them are folded away; **Settings block hidden** above the text opens them.
- **Adding tasks:** the field at the foot says **Add a task…**.
- **Renaming:** press the note's name; links to it follow.
- **Archive** puts a note away. **Delete** moves it to **Deleted**; if it has open reminders, PARAGON asks once whether to remove them too.
- **Share…** hands out a copy as a PDF, plain text or the note's own file. Your note is never touched. A Resource also has a **Notebook** menu (a notebook mark): link a Google NotebookLM notebook to the note, open it, or send a copy of the note to it. On the Mac, PARAGON copies the note and opens the notebook, and you paste it as a source.

### Pictures and sketches

A picture in a note shows in the text. On the iPhone and iPad, bar buttons open a drawing page and add a photo. On the Mac, drag in picture files or paste a screenshot. Hold a picture (right-click on the Mac) for **Copy words**, **Show words** and **Make tasks**: PARAGON reads the words in the picture and can write them out or turn each line into a task.

### Links, tags, templates

- **Links:** type two square brackets and choose a note. **Linked notes** shows what a note points to and what points to it. A link to a note you have not written yet appears under **Not made yet**. On the Mac, Command-click follows a link.
- **Tags:** a tag is one word with a hash in front, like #travel, on a note or task. **Tags…** lists the tags you already have, with counts, so you do not invent a second spelling.
- **Templates** (under **Tools**) are what a new note starts from: Project, Area, Resource, Goal, Daily, Weekly. **Snippets** are ready blocks of tasks (Delegate, Waiting for, Meeting and more) added with the **Snippet** button.

## 7. Goals and aspirations

**Aspirations** and **Goals** each have a row of their own. The difference is the date.

- On an **aspiration**, write **Reason…**, why it matters to you. **Add a goal…** makes a goal that serves it.
- On a **goal**, press **Set a target date…** and **Set a measure…**, one line saying how you will know it is reached, like "All 440 km in 30 days". It also says whether it reaches **This year** or is **Long term**. **Add a project…** makes a project that already serves it.

A project or goal can be **Active**, **On hold**, **Done**, **Missed** (the date passed) or **Dropped** (you chose not to).

### Goals with a number

For goals about a number, like weight or distance: **Set a number…** asks for **Start**, **Target**, **Now** and a **Unit**. A bar shows how far you have come, and **Update…** records today's value.

## 8. Weekly review

A walk down one screen, once a week. The top says how many tasks you finished in the last seven days, with one capsule for each thing that wants a look, or one green capsule, **Nothing needs attention**, when nothing does. **Due for a look** lists notes whose review rhythm has come round: aspirations every 6 months, goals with a date every 3 months, projects every week, areas every month (changeable in **Settings › Review rhythm**). Right-click a row for **I looked at it today**, **Put on hold**, **Mark done**, **Mark missed**, **Mark dropped** or **Archive**.

The steps:

1. **Empty the inbox.**
2. **Reschedule or drop overdue tasks.**
3. **Thin out the loose tasks.** The open tasks nothing else looks at: no date, not waiting, not put aside, not a project's next action. Ten are shown at a time, from the notes you left alone longest. Under each are three answers: **Today**, **Someday** or **Drop**. A few each week is enough.
4. **Read the aspirations again.** Is each still true?
5. **Check the goals with a date are on course.**
6. **Give every project a next action.**
7. **Look over the areas.** An area's **Standard** shows here: your words for what good looks like.
8. **Look back at last week.** Your days, the lines from Close the day, time worked, what ended, and a box, **How was the week?**
9. **Write the goal for the week.** One line; it shows under the date on Today.

The review remembers which parts you left open, and the next visit starts with those. On the Mac, the headings scroll with the list and none sticks to the top (since build 333). Press **Review done** at the end; on the Mac it has room under it. That is enough for the week. Settings can remind you: **Remind me to do the weekly review**.

## 9. Finding things

- **Search:** on the iPhone, **Search notes** at the top of **Browse**; on the Mac, the toolbar field (Shift-Command-F). Type a word, then narrow it with tick boxes for tasks, kinds of note and tags. Writing "done" looks for the word; ticking **Done** asks for finished tasks.
- **Map:** what serves what, goals at the top, then areas, projects and open tasks. A star is an aspiration, a target a goal with a date, a flag a project, four squares an area, books a resource. Click a box to open its note. On the Mac it has its own window (Shift-Command-M).
- **Calendar:** **Day**, **Week**, **Month** and **Notes** (every daily and weekly note you have written). **Week** shows what is due each day; tasks without a date wait in **Not planned yet**, and you drag one onto a day.
- **Recent:** the notes you opened last. On the Mac it is in the sidebar. On the iPhone the newest 5 are at the top of **Browse**, and **All recent notes** under them opens the whole list, with **Clear**.
- **Done:** a diary of the last 12 weeks.
- **Your year:** in **Done**, one page with what you finished in a year. **Share as PDF** is on it.
- **Deleted:** deleted notes, kept for 30 days. **Put back** restores one.
- **Outside PARAGON:** your notes also appear in your device's own search, while **System search** is on in Settings.

## 10. Reminders sync

Apple Reminders puts your tasks in other apps and on your watch. PARAGON and Reminders stay in step in both directions.

What goes across:

- Inbox tasks go to a Reminders list called Inbox.
- Each project and area gets a list with the note's name.
- Tasks in daily and weekly notes go to a list called Daily Notes.
- Ticking, renaming, dating and deleting on either side carries over. A task with a time gets an alarm.
- Goals stay out, because they are direction, not to-dos.

Sync runs by itself every few minutes. On the Mac, click the **sync ring** at the foot of the sidebar for **Sync now**, **Show me what would change…** and the last report. On the iPhone, a tap on the sync button at the top right syncs at once; press and hold it for the same choices. The ring is a small clock: green when the last sync is recent, yellow after a while, red after conflicts, grey before any sync. On the Mac, Shift-Command-R also syncs.

If a task changed on both sides, **Settings › Reminders sync** decides who wins: the note or the reminder.

A task's hidden code (the small mark that links it to its reminder) is worked out from the note and the task's own words, so your Mac and your iPhone always give the same task the same code (since build 342). Before, they could pick different ones in the same minute, and you got a false "changed on two devices" alarm with "(conflict …)" copies. A version that says exactly the same as the note raises no alarm. Different words typed on two devices still do.

When you delete a task on this device, PARAGON starts one quiet sync by itself and the reminder goes within seconds (since build 334): 12 seconds after a delete by a button, a menu or a swipe, and 60 seconds after a line taken out by typing in a note. Several deletes in a row make one sync. There is no message. It does not start when the timed sync is set to never, or when PARAGON may not use Reminders. **Undo** brings the task back and its reminder stays. A line you cut and paste into another note more than a minute later gets a new reminder. Before build 334 (builds 332 and 333) the reminder went at the next sync: the timed sync, **Sync now**, or coming back to the app. Before that, in build 331, it went about 2 minutes later. This covers **Delete** in the Inbox, **Turn into a note**, and a line you take out by typing in a note. If a task disappears any other way, for example through a change that came from another device, its reminder still goes about 2 minutes later (since build 331): the first sync only notes that the task is gone, and a later sync, at least 2 minutes on, deletes the reminder if the task is still in no note. This is why a task you move from one note to another keeps its reminder, even when iCloud brings the two notes in a different order. A line you cut from one note and paste into another can lose its reminder if a sync runs in between; **Move to** does not.

### The questions PARAGON asks

- **A big change:** if one sync would remove many tasks, it shows what it would do. **Do it** goes ahead; **Cancel** changes nothing.
- **Sync stopped to ask you:** a new note has the same name as a Reminders list you already had. **Do it** joins the two, **Cancel** changes nothing, **Keep them apart** leaves both as they are and stops asking.

## 11. Your notes are safe

**Where they live.** Your notes are ordinary files in one folder you choose once in Settings, which says in plain words where it is (iCloud Drive, Dropbox or on the device). On the iPhone and iPad, **Show in Files** opens it; on the Mac, **Show in Finder**.

**Copies.** PARAGON saves a copy of your notes before every sync and once a day, and keeps the recent ones. **Back up now** (Settings › Backups) saves one at once. It also keeps a second copy outside the notes folder, so losing the folder does not lose the copy.

**Restore.** **Restore a copy** and **Restore from the outside copy…** put a copy back, saving what you have first. Notes you made after the copy stay. On the Mac, Time Machine is a good third net.

**The Safety lines.** The top of **Settings** is **Safety**. It only looks. It says **Everything is safe**, or "2 things to look at" in amber. **Details** shows:

- **Devices:** each device and its PARAGON version; it says which to update in TestFlight.
- **Copies inside the notes folder** and **the second copy outside:** amber if the newest is over 2 days old.
- **Conflict copies:** two devices changed a note at once and iCloud kept both.
- **Reminders sync:** when it last ran.

A small amber badge with **!** on the Settings gear (on the Mac, an amber line at the foot of the sidebar) says when something needs a look. The same badge stands in front of each Safety line that needs a look.

## 12. Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, sharing

### Widgets

- **Today's actions:** what is due today or earlier, then next actions. In medium and large sizes, press the circle to tick a task.
- **What is waiting:** four numbers: due today, overdue, in the Inbox, due for a look.
- **Today's plan:** the block you are in, or the next, with a small ruler of the coming hours.
- A wide **Today** widget on the Mac and iPad, and a Lock Screen widget on the iPhone.

On the iPhone, hold the Home Screen and press **+**. On the Mac, choose **Edit Widgets** from the date in the menu bar.

### Siri and Shortcuts

PARAGON has eight actions on the iPhone and the iPad (six on the Mac) in the **Shortcuts** app, which Siri understands with no setup. Each phrase must contain the name PARAGON.

- **Add to Inbox:** "Add to PARAGON". Adds one task without opening the app. In Shortcuts it also has a **Day** field; plain-word dates are not read here.
- **What Is Due Today?:** "What is due today in PARAGON". Answers aloud.
- **Open Today**, **Start the Day**, **Close the Day:** each opens that screen. **Start the Day** opens Today with picking switched on.
- **Start Focus:** "Start focus in PARAGON". With no task named, it starts on what is next today.
- **Record a Thought:** "Record a thought in PARAGON". See **Record a thought** below.
- **Record into Today's Note** (iPhone and iPad, since build 349): "Record into today's note in PARAGON" or "Today's note recording in PARAGON". The same, but the thought goes into today's note. See **Record a thought** below.

### Record a thought

Since build 348. iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or newer; on the Mac the action is not offered (on an older phone Siri says "Record a thought needs iOS 26 or newer."). The action **Record a Thought** is in Shortcuts under PARAGON, with Siri ("Record a thought in PARAGON", "Record in PARAGON", "Voice note in PARAGON") and on the Action Button (iPhone 15 Pro or newer: Settings app › Action Button › Shortcut › PARAGON › Record a Thought). Before the first press, open PARAGON › Settings › **Voice** and press **Get ready** once (since build 353): it asks for the microphone and gets the speech model, which a press from the Action Button cannot do. Live Activities must be on for PARAGON too (since build 354 Settings › **Voice** shows it): iOS stops a recording in the background that does not show on the Lock Screen. If a press does nothing, look at **What the last presses did** at the foot of Settings › **Voice**. Since build 355 a press opens PARAGON for the start (iOS does not let an app in the background start the Lock Screen card); after that you can leave PARAGON or lock the phone, and the recording goes on. A short message at the foot of PARAGON says "Recording. Press again to stop." A task the AI finds is kept only when at least half of its longer words are words you said (since build 357).

- **Press once:** recording starts at once, also with the phone locked, with a short tap. **Press again**, or the red **Stop** button on the Lock Screen: it stops. The longest recording is 30 minutes; then it stops and saves by itself. The first time, iOS asks to use the microphone and speech recognition.
- **Into today's note (since build 349):** the action **Record into Today's Note** works the same but files into today's note; it can be on the Action Button instead of Record a Thought (Settings app › Action Button › Shortcut › PARAGON › Record into Today's Note); press again to stop. A long press cannot choose (Apple's Action Button sends one kind of press), so the Lock Screen card has the choice.
- **While recording:** the Lock Screen card shows a red dot, "Listening · Inbox" or "Listening · Today's note", the running time (0:42) and the last words you said, live. The Dynamic Island shows a red dot and the time; expanded, the live words and **Stop**. Since build 349 there are two buttons: the red **Stop** files where the heading says; the second stops and files to the other place: **Today** (purple, sun mark) when it says Inbox, **Inbox** (green, tray mark) when it says today's note. Once a task is caught the card shows "1 task heard" / "N tasks heard" before the live words. The first recording in a language may show "Getting the speech model ready…" while Apple's speech model is fetched once.
- **After stopping:** "Writing it up…", then "Saved to Inbox" with the tray mark (or "Saved to today's note" with the sun mark) and the title and "N tasks found". The card goes away after a few minutes.
- **Saved as a note:** a new note in **Resources**, titled by the phone, tagged #voice, with "## Summary" (two or three sentences), "## Tasks" (the tasks found, as plain list lines) and "## Full text" (everything you said), plus the day, time and length.
- **Saved in the Inbox:** each task found as its own open task line, and one line under Notes that links to the new note, for example "[[Drawer screws and Saturday]] — voice note, 0:42". Sort them like any Inbox line.
- **Saved in today's note (since build 349):** the note still goes to Resources; the tasks become open tasks in today's daily note under Tasks (so they count on Today); the line "[[Title]] — voice note, 0:42" goes under Notes of today's daily note; nothing goes into the Inbox. A summary that comes late puts its new tasks into that same day's note.
- **Say "task" (since build 349):** a sentence that starts with "task" (also "new task"; German "Aufgabe", "neue Aufgabe"; Swedish "uppgift", "ny uppgift") becomes a task without that word: "Task: call Anna about Saturday." gives "Call Anna about Saturday". The word alone ("Task. Order screws.") makes the next sentence the task. Only at the start of a sentence ("This task is hard" is not one). At most 25 words. Always filed, even without Apple Intelligence or when the summary is late; they come first and the AI's tasks that say the same or less are left out.
- **Who does the work:** title, summary and tasks come from Apple Intelligence on the phone; speech to text is Apple's too. It is free, private and works without internet.
- **Without Apple Intelligence** (turned off, or an older iPhone): the text is still saved, the title is the first words, no summary and no tasks are made, and the note says so in one line.
- **When the phone is locked.** The iPhone slows Apple's AI down while it is locked. If the summary is not ready after 20 seconds, the words are saved at once anyway, with the first words as the title, and the note says "The summary is still being written. PARAGON adds it the next time it opens." The next time you open PARAGON, the summary goes into the note and the tasks into the Inbox. Nothing you said waits or gets lost.
- **Nothing heard:** nothing is saved; the card says "Nothing heard — nothing saved".
- **Settings › Voice › Voice language:** "Phone's language" (default), English, Deutsch or Svenska. Under it: "The language you speak when you record a thought."
- **Signs:** a red dot on the Lock Screen or in the Dynamic Island means PARAGON is recording your voice right now. The second button: sun = into today's note, tray = into the Inbox. "N tasks heard" = spoken tasks caught so far. The microphone mark on a Shortcut tile is **Record a Thought**.

**More for voice notes (since build 350).**
- **Words you say:** dates ("tomorrow", "on Friday", "the 25th") and times ("at 3:30", "um 15 Uhr") become the task's date. "Remind me" makes a task. "Tag garden" adds a tag, "Idea …" lists an idea, "Title …" at the start names the note, and "stop recording" at the end stops and files.
- **Pause and Mark** are buttons on the Lock Screen card. **Mark** marks the moment; that sentence is shown in bold under **Marked**.
- **Check after filing** (Settings › Voice, on): the thought is filed at once, and then a card lets you change the title and switch tasks off. **Throw it away** moves the note to **Deleted** for 30 days.
- **Voice notes** (under **Look back** on the Mac, in **Browse › Plan** on the iPhone) lists every recording. Each row can **Read aloud**, **Write it up again** or be thrown away.
- **Words it mishears** (Settings › Voice) teaches PARAGON your names. **Read the summary aloud** and **Summary in English** are in Settings › Voice too.
- **Control Center:** you can add **Capture** and **Record a Thought** as buttons. They open PARAGON, so the phone must be unlocked.

### Sharing into PARAGON

In Safari, Photos, Files, Mail and others, press **Share** and choose PARAGON. It takes text, links, and up to 20 pictures and PDFs at once. Choose **Inbox** or **Today's note**, switch on **As note, not task** if you want a note, and press **Save**. PARAGON files the share the next time it comes to the front, so nothing is lost if iCloud is slow.

## 13. Keys on the Mac

- **Command-N** new note; **Shift-Command-N** quick capture.
- **Shift-Command-F** search everywhere.
- **Shift-Command-R** sync with Reminders.
- **Command-1** to **Command-7** go to Inbox, Today, Plan, All actions, Aspirations, Projects and Weekly review.
- **Shift-Command-P** Plan in its own window; **Shift-Command-M** the Map.
- **Control-Command-Left Arrow** and **Right Arrow** go back and forward between notes.
- **Escape** is Cancel in any window where you change something; **Command-S** saves.

Every detail is in Settings › Every detail.
